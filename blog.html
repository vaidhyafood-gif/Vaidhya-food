Sourcing

Choosing a Bulk Paneer Supplier in Chhattisgarh: What to Check

For hotels and caterers in Raipur, Bhilai, and Bilaspur, picking a paneer supplier isn't just about price. Consistency matters more — a supplier whose paneer cooks the same way every time saves your kitchen from last-minute recipe adjustments. Look for FSSAI certification, a clear cold-chain process from farm to plant, and a track record with commercial kitchens rather than only retail counters. Ask for cube-retention performance in gravies, not just a spec sheet — how paneer holds up in a Kadai Paneer or Paneer Butter Masala service is the real test. Vaidhya Foods works directly with milk collection partners across Chhattisgarh, giving 5000+ kitchens a supply they can plan a menu around.

Quality

What FSSAI Certification Actually Means for Your Kitchen

An FSSAI State License isn't just paperwork — it signals a manufacturer has been assessed against defined hygiene, processing, and traceability standards under the FSS Act, 2006. For institutional buyers like hostels and hospitals, this is often a procurement requirement, not a nice-to-have. Vaidhya Foods holds FSSAI State License No. 10526013000033, covering manufacturing, distribution, wholesale, and insulated/refrigerated transportation. That last part matters more than most buyers realise: a certified product that travels in an uncooled vehicle loses the benefit of that certification by the time it reaches your kitchen door.

Cold Chain

How We Keep Paneer Fresh From Plant to Kitchen

Paneer is unforgiving about temperature. A break in the cold chain — even for a few hours in transit — shows up as texture loss, faster spoilage, and inconsistent cooking results. At Vaidhya Foods, product is stored at 4°C from the moment it's packed, moved in insulated and refrigerated vehicles, and tracked through to delivery across Chhattisgarh and Odisha. For kitchens ordering in bulk — 50kg MOQ and above — that consistency is the difference between planning a menu with confidence and constantly adjusting for supply variability.
