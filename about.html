Our Story

FROM A SINGLE PLANT TO 5000+ KITCHENS

With a strong focus on quality, hygiene, and customer satisfaction, we serve hotels, restaurants, caterers, distributors, retailers, and institutional buyers with products designed to meet the demands of modern food service operations.

Today, Vaidhya Foods is trusted by over 5000+ kitchens — not just for the products we manufacture, but for the consistency, reliability, and long-term partnerships we build. As we continue to expand our manufacturing capabilities and distribution network, our commitment remains unchanged: delivering quality products that businesses can depend on every day.
